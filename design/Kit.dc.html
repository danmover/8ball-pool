The Kit
Four rules hold it together. Surfaces are oiled wood with a lit top edge. Anything you press is a moulded button with a visible lip, and pressing pushes it into that lip. Colour is spent, not spread — brass means press this. And the table, the cloth and the balls are the game's own, pixel for pixel: those values come out of tableThemes.ts and ballStyle.ts, not out of a mood board.
